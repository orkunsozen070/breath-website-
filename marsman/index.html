    ● How it plays out

    Two acts and an ending you choose.

    Act I · The signal

    Get a message to Earth.

    Rebuild the Wayfarer's deep-space transmitter, seven parts in all. Find your crew across the canyons, the lava tubes and the ice. Then send the signal home.

    Earth replies: rescue in 2 years · room for 4

    Act II · The Ancient Road

    Build a ship for everyone.

    Under the surface lies the Depths, a city the Ancients left when they went to the stars. Their archive marks a road: clear the wreck, raise a shipyard, enter the Dead Colony, and take a core from each of seven guardians.

    Seven cores · one starship

    Ending · Your call

    Fly home, or stay.

    Board the starship with the survivors who want Earth, or stay with the ones who now call Mars home. Either way, the world keeps running after the credits.

    Free play continues after the end

    ● Survival systems

    Six ways Mars tries to kill you.

    Oxygen O₂

    Every breath outside the dome comes out of your tank. You start with five spare cartridges. Upgrade the tank, or learn where the oxygen springs are.

    Spare cartridges at start · 5

    Water & food H₂O

    Dig ice and melt it. Grow vegetables in the greenhouse, hunt, cook. A full stomach and a full flask bring your health back fast.

    Health regen when fed · ×6

    Body heat °C

    Frozen nights and volcanic heat both drain you. Heaters, insulated suit linings and a cooling module keep your core where it belongs.

    Volcanic Ridge without cooling · O₂ ×1.7

    Dust & meteors SKY

    Dust storms close in without warning. Meteor showers light up the night and burn whatever they hit, you included.

    Watch them from the observatory

    The horde 8–11 SOLS

    Once the village grows, a swarm comes every eight to eleven sols. You get a warning at dawn. That night it hits in three waves and breaks what it reaches.

    Repair damaged buildings by hand

    Night 19:30–05:30

    After dark the plains belong to the creatures. Fire a flare and they will not step into the light. Or skip ahead to morning.

    ● Build · From scrap shelter to sealed dome

    A home
    under glass.

    Start with a shelter made of scrap. End with a glass-domed village: a ring road, a fountain at every junction, a greenhouse, an orchard and a waterfall fed from the Silent Sea.

    1. 01

      Construction in real time

      Builds keep going while the app is closed. A shelter takes 30 seconds. The shipyard takes four hours.

    2. 02

      Engineers and a cargo drone

      Two engineers raise every wall while a drone hauls the crates. More crew means more builds at once.

    3. 03

      Magnetic parcels

      Plots snap to the street and doors turn to face the road, so the village lays itself out around you.

    1. 04

      Power through the night

      Solar panels feed the grid, batteries carry it after sunset. Turrets draw power too, so plan before you upgrade.

    2. 05

      Defenses that grow

      Crossbow turrets become lasers. The fence holds the gates, and the gates mend themselves between raids.

    3. 06

      Neighbors

      Survivors you bring back move in, tell you about the colony they lost, and help the village grow.
