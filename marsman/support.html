MarsMan for iPhone

Stuck on Mars? Here are the answers to the most common questions. If yours is not here, write to us.

Does MarsMan need an internet connection?

No. The whole game, story and village included, works offline.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. The game saves automatically while you play and when you leave the app. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start a new game?

On the main menu, choose NEW GAME. Your previous save is replaced, so make sure you want to start over.

Why do my buildings keep building while the app is closed?

Construction, the village dome, village projects and crops run on real time. Come back later and the work will have moved on without you. The clock button next to the time of day skips to morning or evening.

My Game Center achievements are not showing.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then open the game again. Achievements you unlocked earlier are sent when you sign in.

What language is the game in?

The game's text is currently in Turkish.
