Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Thank you for using Cashly. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This policy explains how our app handles your data.

1. Data Collected

Cashly stores users' financial data only on the device and in the user's own iCloud account. We do not collect any personal data.

  • Financial data (income, expenses, budgets, investment information): Stored only on your device and in your iCloud account.
  • Authentication: When Apple Sign In or Google Sign In is used, only basic profile information (name, email) is processed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics may be collected through Firebase Analytics (e.g., screen views).

2. Use of Data

Collected data is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing the app's core functionality (financial tracking, budget management)
  • Enabling cross-device synchronization via iCloud
  • Improving the app experience (anonymous analytics)

3. Data Sharing

Your personal data is never shared with or sold to third parties. The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and anonymous analytics
  • Apple iCloud: Data synchronization (CloudKit)
  • CoinGecko, Binance, Yahoo Finance API: Real-time price information (no personal data is sent)

4. Data Security

Your data is stored encrypted using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. The app can be protected with biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID). All API communication occurs over HTTPS.

5. User Rights

At any time, you can:

  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Delete Cashly data from your iCloud settings
  • Close your Firebase account to delete your authentication data

6. Children's Privacy

Cashly is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
