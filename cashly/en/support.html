Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. All your financial data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account. We do not collect any personal data.

Is the app free?

Yes, Cashly is completely free to use.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete all local data by removing the app from your device. To delete iCloud data, go to Settings > Your Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Does it sync across devices?

Yes, when you're signed in with your iCloud account, your data automatically syncs across all your Apple devices.

Why aren't investment prices updating?

Price data is fetched from CoinGecko, Binance, and Yahoo Finance APIs. Check your internet connection and pull down to refresh the app.
