Inside the app

Scroll, and let Cashly talk.

With every step below, the phone moves through real screens of the app.

01 — OVERVIEW

Your balance, banknote-sharp

The engraved overview card shows your balance, income–expense flow and savings rate at a glance. Finance news and your assets line up right below.

02 — TRANSACTIONS

Every penny, on record

Categorize income and spending, scroll back through a day-grouped history, and filter or search with one tap.

03 — DISCOVER

News, in your language

Follow the markets without leaving the app. Headlines arrive in the app's language — Turkish users read Turkish financial press, others get Yahoo Finance.

04 — BUDGET

See it before you spend it

Set per-category limits and track the month on the ring gauge. Recurring payments like rent and subscriptions post themselves when due.

05 — INVESTMENTS

Your portfolio, at live prices

Track crypto, forex and commodities at live rates; see your P&L and allocation on a single panel. Savings goals live here too.

06 — REPORT

The month's story, in charts

Weekly, monthly and yearly reports cover your cash flow, category breakdown and savings rate. Share your monthly summary as an elegant email report.
